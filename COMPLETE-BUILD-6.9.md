# myHabbit 6.9 — Telegram-only login

- Telegram login is available only inside a real Telegram WebApp session.
- The Telegram login button and related recovery text are completely absent in browsers, installed PWA, Windows and non-Telegram WebViews.
- Browser/PWA account recovery remains available through encrypted JSON.
- External invocation of the Telegram login action is blocked.
- Root and public assets are synchronized; cache version raised to 6.9.
